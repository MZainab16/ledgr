What is Ledgr?

Bookkeeping that starts with a photo

Ledgr is an AI receipt scanner that turns paper receipts, invoices and order slips into organised Google Sheets. Instead of typing every item, price and total by hand, you upload a photo or PDF and Ledgr does the data entry for you in seconds.

Every receipt is read line by line: the business name, date, receipt number, each item with its quantity and price, the tax, the total and how it was paid. Ledgr then saves it to two clean tabs in your Google Sheet, one row per receipt and one row per item, so your numbers are ready to sort, filter and add up.

Your receipts also become searchable. Ask Ledgr's AI assistant questions in plain English, like "What did I buy from my supplier last week?" or "How much have I spent on fuel this month?", and get answers from your own receipts.

FAQ

Questions about Ledgr

What is Ledgr?

Ledgr is an AI receipt scanner for businesses. You upload a photo or PDF of a receipt, invoice or order slip, and Ledgr reads every line, organises it into a clean table, and saves it to your Google Sheets.

How do I turn receipts into a Google Sheet?

Sign up on Ledgr, upload a receipt photo or PDF, tap Extract information, then tap Send to Google Sheets. Ledgr creates a sheet with a Receipts tab and an Items tab, or adds to a sheet you made with Ledgr before.

Is Ledgr free?

Yes, you can start free. Every account gets 3 free receipts and 20 AI questions a month. Ledgr Pro costs ₦5,000 a month and includes 100 receipts and 50 AI questions every month.

What kinds of receipts can Ledgr read?

Ledgr reads printed shop receipts, POS slips, invoices, supplier bills and order slips, as photos (JPG, PNG, WEBP) or PDFs. Clear, well lit photos give the best results.

Can Ledgr see my whole Google Drive?

No. Ledgr only asks Google for access to files it creates itself, so it can see and edit the sheets made through Ledgr and nothing else in your Drive.

Does Ledgr work with Naira and Nigerian receipts?

Yes. Ledgr is built for Nigerian businesses. It understands Naira amounts and reads dates day first, the way Nigerian receipts print them, and it also handles other currencies.

How do I pay for Ledgr Pro?

Ledgr Pro is paid monthly through Paystack by card, bank transfer or USSD. You can cancel anytime and keep Pro until the end of the month you paid for.
