What is Ledgr?

Paper records in, spreadsheets out

Ledgr is an AI record scanner that turns photos of written or printed information into organised spreadsheets. Receipts, invoices, handwritten sales books, stock counts, attendance sheets, customer lists, price lists: if it's written down, Ledgr can put it in rows and columns.

Ledgr copies what it sees, exactly as written. It keeps your own titles and column headings in the same order, without adding or removing anything. The only extras are the date it was extracted and a note for anything it couldn't read clearly. Want something different? Just tell Ledgr when you upload, for example "only include the Name and Amount columns".

Save each record to Google Sheets or download it as an Excel file. Then ask Ledgr's AI assistant questions in plain English, like "How much did Mrs Ada buy this month?", and get answers from your own records.

FAQ

Questions about Ledgr

What is Ledgr?

Ledgr is an AI record scanner. You upload a photo or PDF of a receipt, a handwritten record, an invoice or any written information, and Ledgr copies every entry into rows and columns, keeping your own titles. You can save it to Google Sheets or download it as an Excel file.

What kinds of records can Ledgr read?

Ledgr reads printed receipts, invoices, POS slips, handwritten sales books, stock counts, attendance sheets, customer lists, price lists and most other written or printed records, as photos (JPG, PNG, WEBP) or PDFs. Clear, well lit photos give the best results.

Does Ledgr change or add to my data?

No. Ledgr copies entries exactly as written, using the same column titles and order as your record. It only adds the date the record was extracted and a note for anything it could not read clearly. If you want changes, such as skipping a column, just tell Ledgr when you upload.

How do I turn a paper record into a Google Sheet or Excel file?

Sign up on Ledgr, upload a photo or PDF of the record, and tap Extract information. Then tap Send to Google Sheets, or Download Excel to get an .xlsx file. Records with the same layout go into the same tab.

Is Ledgr free?

Yes, you can start free. Every account gets 3 free records and 20 AI questions a month. Ledgr Pro costs ₦5,000 a month for 100 records and 50 AI questions every month.

Can Ledgr see my whole Google Drive?

No. Ledgr only asks Google for access to files it creates itself, so it can see and edit the sheets made through Ledgr and nothing else in your Drive.

Does Ledgr work with Nigerian records?

Yes. Ledgr is built for Nigerian businesses. It reads Naira amounts and day-first dates like 01/07/2026 as 1 July, and handles both handwriting and printed text.

How do I pay for Ledgr Pro?

Ledgr Pro is paid monthly by card through Paystack and renews automatically. You can cancel anytime and keep Pro until the end of the month you paid for.
